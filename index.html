Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At ChronosCalendar Pro, your privacy is our core priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines how data handles within your browser interface.

1. Client-Side Operations

All calendar rendering, calendar node selections, and high-precision time calculations run locally inside your browser runtime. We do not track, collect, transmit, or store your target date selections or schedule parameters on any external remote server database.

2. Standard Server Logs

As standard for static web repositories (like GitHub Pages), basic technical metadata including browser type, timestamps, and standard routing requests may be captured automatically by hosting edge network infrastructure for reliability analysis.

3. Cookies Policy

We do not deploy invasive marketing trackers, session profiling cookies, or third-party behavioral beacons.
