Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At ChronosPulse, accessible from our GitHub Pages deployment, your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by ChronosPulse and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Processing

All time calculation routines, scripts, and inputs execute locally within your web browser. We do not transmit, track, capture, or store your target start dates, end dates, or custom inputs on any remote web server.

2. Log Files

ChronosPulse follows a standard procedure of using hosting log files (such as GitHub Pages). These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring pages. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising identifiers to track user activity.
